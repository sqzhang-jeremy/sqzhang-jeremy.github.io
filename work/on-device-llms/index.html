Context makes a difference.

This research demo combines on-device language models with smartphone sensing to explore context-aware, personalised services.

Keeping intelligence close.

The framework brings together sensor data and prompt engineering on a smartphone. A university-student case study demonstrates tailored recommendations, while the research examines trade-offs around privacy, latency, cost and energy consumption.

THE IDEA

Personal context + local intelligence → More relevant assistance

A starting point for further research.

This is a research demonstration. Broader sensor inputs and more extensive user studies are identified as future work.
