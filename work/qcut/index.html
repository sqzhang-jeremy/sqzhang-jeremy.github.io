From conversation to a finished video.

FIST REALM was generated entirely through natural-language interactions with QCut’s chat agent. I contributed prompts and tested the agent to help co-create the video.

The project brings together my interests in filmmaking and AI agents: shaping a creative result through conversation and testing how the agent responds.

Tools that let agents work with video.

At QCut, I designed and shipped an agent CLI that enables AI agents to inspect projects, manipulate video timelines, generate and transcribe media, and export videos.

Exploring the next workflows.

I’m experimenting with and developing audio search and automatic video-cutting features, alongside contributing prompts and testing QCut’s chat agent.
