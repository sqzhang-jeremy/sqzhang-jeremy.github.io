Connecting the dots.

My path has taken me from mechanical engineering to computer science, from medical-image models to language models, and from research experiments to building ParkingMate.ai.

I completed an MPhil in Computer Science at the University of Melbourne. My research explores mobile intelligence: evaluating agents that interact with apps, and using smartphone sensing to make on-device language models more personal.

I’ve also worked as a Head Tutor at the University of Melbourne, mentoring Android project teams and teaching website development with generative AI tools.

What connects these experiences is a practical question: how do we make technology useful, understandable and dependable for the people using it?
